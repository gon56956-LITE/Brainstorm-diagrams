Observed issue: the unit intermittently fails to complete cold startup after overnight storage. The failure appears after low-temperature soak and usually clears after a full power cycle. It has been seen on engineering samples from two assembly lots, but not on every unit.

Evidence so far:
- Input voltage droop was captured during inrush on two failing samples.
- A power-good signal is sometimes delayed long enough that the controller does not enter its normal boot sequence.
- Firmware logs sometimes stop before the ready handshake.
- The start signal is generated only when the power-good line is stable and the controller exits boot.
- Harness inspection found intermittent connector seating on one lot.
- Thermal chamber records show the issue is more likely after cold soak than after room-temperature storage.

The review goal is to break down possible logical causes for the top event, not to prove a final root cause yet. Use AND only where the source says conditions must happen together.
